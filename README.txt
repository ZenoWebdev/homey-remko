Keep your home comfortable with local control of a REMKO Smart-Control Touch heat pump. See room, outdoor and water temperatures, adjust the room target and climate mode, and use the built-in forecast in Flows.

Use surplus power reported by another Homey app to charge hot water at the right time. The app can also manage a scheduled hygiene cycle and remembers the last cycle it has observed. A REMKO Smart-Control Touch on the same local network is required. This is an independent community integration.
