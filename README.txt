Keep your home comfortable with local control of a REMKO Smart-Control Touch heat pump. See room, outdoor and water temperatures and the current operating state, adjust the room target and climate mode, and use the built-in forecast in Flows.

Use surplus power reported by another Homey app to charge hot water at the right time. The app also manages a scheduled hygiene cycle. Tested on a REMKO WKF 80 with Smart Web; other REMKO models using the same interface are expected to work but are not yet verified. Please share your model and experience via the Support link. This is an independent community integration.
